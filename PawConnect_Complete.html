Need help for an animal?

Report a case

Use the Google Form to share the details with the project team. Add the most accurate location you can, such as an area, landmark or map pin.

  • Keep a safe distance from frightened or injured animals.
  • Do not attempt to move a seriously injured animal unless advised by a trained rescuer.
  • Include a phone number so the team can follow up.
Open animal report form ↗

The form opens in a new tab. If it does not load, check that its Google Forms access is set to accept responses.
